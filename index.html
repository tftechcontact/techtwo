we want agency

The idea here is that there's a plethora of options to build websites and it's hard to choose the most feasible option. There are plenty of platforms where the customer can move features around on the screen with a click of the mouse but those options are limited with only choosing out of a handful of fonts or not being able to choose the background color of website. Plus, on top of that the prices can range from $18.00 monthly subscription fees up to $200 with upgrades and surprise fees. But where is the option for the small business that just wants a one page website showcasing some examples of their work and making a professional platform to entice potential customers. Why should that business owner or freelancer who would only get 10,000 visitors to their page per month pay the same as someone who is getting 50,000 visitors because that's a big difference in bandwidth. We want a sense that we have control over our money and to not be swindled. I want to help assist people and provide a block of code for each customer along with assistance in signing up for a vetted hosting platform that costs $5-10 per month, or less. There's just no need to spend more for less.
